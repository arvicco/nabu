The Indian epigraphic record arrived in force. The DHARMA project's
Tamil-country estate — eleven corpora from the Pallava charters to
the Chola temple walls, the Pāṇḍya country, and the South Indian
Inscriptions selections — enters as 1,267 editions read at the line
grain, the inscriptions' own romanized Tamil with the engraver's
line breaks kept. Beside it the Deccan estate: twenty corpora from
Early Āndhradeśa and Kanaganahalli's Prakrit donor records through
the Telugu and Kalyāṇa Cālukya stones to the Maitraka and Bengal
copper plates — 1,694 editions more, every in-file license recorded
verbatim and every licence-less file carrying its repository's
grant honestly. A metadata sidecar of 734 rows brings the estates'
dating and findspot scholarship along as data.

The Siddham archive joined from its Zenodo deposit: 427 Gupta,
Vākāṭaka and Kadamba inscriptions, and with them the library's
first shared era table — Śaka, Vikrama, Kollam and Gupta-Valabhī
offsets drawn from Sewell and Dikshit's 1896 tables — so that a
donation dated "GE 98" now carries its real envelope of 417–418 CE.

Literature came with the stones. Andrew Ollett's editions bring the
first Kannada literary texts the library has ever held — sixteen
classics from Pampa's Ādipurāṇa of 941 through Ranna, Nāgavarma II
and Janna to Kanakadāsa, their meters annotated — and twenty-two
licensed Prakrit works: Hāla's Sattasaī, the Gaüḍavaho, Vimala's
Paumacariya, the Deśīnāmamālā, and two Śvētāmbara canonical texts
whose Ardhamāgadhī is the first individual Prakrit the library
names as its own language. Nine canonical files whose editions
carry no grant stay out, excluded by a parse-time license gate
rather than by silence.

The registry grew to carry all of it: Telugu and Kannada anchors
under their Dravidian families, Old and Middle Kannada stages from
the Halmidi inscription's fifth century to the dāsa poets, Tamil
re-seated on the South Dravidian branch, and Ardhamāgadhī minted
under the Prakrit collective — four language shelves lit at once,
every claim resolving on a registry node and zero pending postures
left on the board.

As always: canonical means canonical — every text verbatim from its
source, every exclusion a written rule, every license the grantor's
own words.
