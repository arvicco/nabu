## Who is behind Nabu

Nabu is built and maintained by one person, writing as **Ar Vicco**
&lt;[arvicco@nabu.ac](mailto:arvicco@nabu.ac)&gt;.

**A long-time ancient-language enthusiast.** The library did not start as
a software project; it started as a reading habit that kept outgrowing
its bookshelf. Nabu is what that habit looks like once it insists on
holding its sources, citing them properly, and searching them across
scripts and millennia.

**Independent, by design.** The maintainer holds no academic affiliation
and earns a living as a systems architect in the commercial space. Nabu
is built in personal time, with the engineering habits of that day job —
tests first, licenses recorded, everything rebuildable — applied to a
scholarly library. Independence is also why the project's commitments
run the way they do: no institutional hosting, no funding horizon to
outlive, nothing that cannot be restored from a file copy.

**An independent private researcher.** The research interest behind the
library is twofold: **writing systems** — how scripts encode language,
how they are deciphered, [taught](https://arvicco.github.io/nabu-edubba/), and change hands — and the
**statistical and diachronic properties of languages**, ancient and
modern: how vocabularies, spellings, and grammatical habits drift
across centuries, and what becomes measurable when enough texts sit in
one place under one set of tools. The [research desks]({{ '/axis/' | relative_url }})
and the [language-stage machinery]({{ '/languages/' | relative_url }})
are instruments of those two questions, and the published
[nabu-data](https://github.com/arvicco/nabu-data) datasets carry the
first results — for the lexicostatistical side, the
[diachronic lemma-frequency tables](https://github.com/arvicco/nabu-data/tree/main/mul/lemma-frequencies)
(gold lemma frequencies per language at century grain) and the
[language attestation curve](https://github.com/arvicco/nabu-data/tree/main/mul/language-curve)
(documents and passages per language × century × corpus).

Questions and conversation are welcome — by
[email](mailto:arvicco@nabu.ac) or through
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/arvicco/nabu/issues); the
[About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) page describes the project
itself.
